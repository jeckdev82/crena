How it works

Three steps to keep your money under control

Crena is built for people who already tried tracking expenses in Excel or in their notes and gave up after a week.

1

Log every expense in 2 seconds

Name, amount, done: the category is suggested automatically. Frequent expenses — coffee, bread, gas — you save with one tap from the widget, without opening the app.

2

Set your monthly budget

Decide how much you want to spend this month. Crena calculates what you have left, your daily average and tells you whether you're on track until the end of the month — before it's too late.

3

Discover where your money goes

Statistics by category, top expenses of the month, balance trend and a day-by-day calendar. Subscriptions and recurring payments are already included in the monthly impact.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free app to track expenses on iPhone?
Crena is a free iPhone app designed precisely to log expenses in seconds: type the expense, the amount, and the category is suggested automatically. It includes a monthly budget, statistics, a calendar and subscription management. Learn more →
How do I keep track of my daily expenses?
The method that works is logging the expense the moment you make it — later, you'll forget. With Crena it takes two seconds, and frequent expenses can be saved with one tap from the widget without opening the app. Learn more →
How do I set a monthly budget?
You set a spending limit for the month and Crena calculates what you have left, your daily average and whether you're on track until the end of the month, with a progress bar always visible on the Home screen. Learn more →
Does Crena connect to my bank account?
No, by design: no bank access, no sign-up. Your data stays on your iPhone, and the app works with cash and with any bank. Learn more →
How does the 50/30/20 rule work?
You split your net income into 50% needs, 30% wants and 20% savings. With Crena's budget and categories you can apply it and check it month after month. Learn more →
Can I manage subscriptions and recurring payments?
Yes: subscriptions, rent and salary log themselves when they're due, and Crena shows you the estimated monthly impact of all your recurring transactions. Learn more →
Can I export my expenses?
You can save a full backup of your data anytime; with Crena Premium you can also export an expense statement, handy for your records or for a spreadsheet. Learn more →
How much does Crena cost?
Crena is free: expenses, monthly budget, monthly statistics, calendar and widget included. Premium ($1.99/month or $9.99/year) adds unlimited recurring transactions, statement export and advanced statistics. Download free →
